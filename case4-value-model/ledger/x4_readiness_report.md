## Young cars: read the early months with care

The engine's layer 1 for the group's brands runs well above the whole market's at the youngest ages (`analysis/readiness_base_hazard.csv`), which fits dealer self-registrations passing to customers (`analysis/self_registration_report.md`), not customers replacing. So a young car's score overstates customer readiness. The queue therefore starts at the equity window, month 18 (`window_nl_measured_m`); the age-only queue, from age one, stays in the store beside it. The latest queue holds only older cars, so the two agree on it.

## Checks

- pass: one score per car per month from age one to the book's last month
- pass: no car has two scores for one month
- pass: five cars' 180 scores asked of the engine directly (worst gap 0.0e+00)
- pass: every month: only retail cars past the equity window (month 18) flagged, at least the top tenth of that queue, every flagged car above every unflagged one
- pass: the window queue and the age-only queue hold the same scores, row for row
- pass: the age-only queue's rows are untouched
- pass: the queue's population is the registration record's private and conquest buyers
- pass: facts and decisions unchanged
- pass: marks unchanged
- pass: running the last month again adds nothing
- pass: the last month's score is unknown the day before its month end
