## What it shows

- **A returned young car loses -8.4% of its value a year to age alone at fixed mileage** (cars 0-2 years, median across eight markets; -8.2% at 2-4 years). Over all ages the engine's mean, -8.8%, sits inside the register's pooled range.
- **The market's average drift offsets part of it:** the core markets' indices rose by a median 0.07% a month over 2016-2025 and -0.05% before the shortage. That is an average, not a forecast (X7).
- **A day in stock costs about EUR 2.86 to EUR 5.19 per EUR 10,000 of young car** (central EUR 3.07), counting ageing, the average drift and financing.
- **On a year's buy-back returns (EUR 5,313m), each day of average time to sale costs about EUR 1.5m to EUR 2.8m** (central EUR 1.6m). Ten days cut is ten times that. How many days the group can cut is not measured: it discloses no days to sell.
- **One car at Aramis's latest realised price** (EUR 16,589, FY26 Q3) loses about EUR 3.97 a day to age, and costs EUR 4.74 to EUR 8.60 a day in all (central EUR 5.09). A German dealer's standing day is EUR 30 before any loss of value (DAT): a dealer's day is mostly the cost of running the stock, the group's mostly the car ageing and its financing, so a dealer's day plus the ageing is the upper reference for a retail sale.

## Limits

- **Advert prices** and the engine's curve; the loss a remarketer meets at auction may differ.
- **The curve bends in some markets only.** Where the engine's local level doesn't change with age (AT, FR, NL and PT in the table), the yearly cost is the straight-line slope at every age. It is measured at fixed mileage, so it is flatter for young cars than a curve that also counts their driving.
- **The drift is an average over a window with a boom in it** (hence the pre-shortage reading); a given month's move is risk, priced by X6, not an expected cost.
- **The book is a payable:** a car's value at return is taken as its repurchase price.
- **Days saved are not measured here.** The group discloses none; part 5 anchors an assumption to DAT, INDICATA and Auto Trader.
